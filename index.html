Thesis Research

Performance Comparison of CoAtNet-0 and EfficientNet-B0 Architectures in Corn Leaf Disease Classification

  • Compared two deep learning architectures across four corn leaf disease classes using 4,354 images.
  • Built EfficientNet-B0 with ImageNet transfer learning and CoAtNet-0 from scratch.
  • Evaluated precision, recall, F1-score, and confusion matrix performance.
  • Ran an out-of-distribution robustness test against 1,143 irrelevant background images.
  • EfficientNet-B0 reached 97.31% testing accuracy against CoAtNet-0 at 94%.
